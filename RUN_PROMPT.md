# DAB — Reusable Outreach Run Prompt

Copy/paste this template into ChatGPT, filling in the brackets.

> **DAB OUTREACH RUN**
>
> Prepare **[NUMBER: e.g., 20, 30, 70] new Gmail drafts** for **[INDUSTRY/INDUSTRIES]** in **[COUNTRY AND OPTIONAL REGION/CITIES]**.
>
> Use GitHub repo `agads007-sys/dentists-germany` as the DAB workspace. Read `README.md` and `MESSAGE.md` first and follow their instructions.
>
> **Recipient rules:** Find independent, active businesses with a plausible review-collection need. Prioritize a **named owner, partner, director, or relevant manager** with a verifiable **direct, publicly listed professional email**. Exclude generic/role addresses (e.g., info@, contact@, office@, mail@, support@, service@, admin@, reception@, praxis@, hello@ and comparable aliases). Do not guess names or email formats. Skip a business if a suitable direct address is unavailable.
>
> **Email rules:** Write in natural local language, with a short truthful subject, roughly 75–115 words, one verified relevant observation, a clear explanation of the DAB SMS review-request service, and one simple question. Do not criticize review counts, use generic SEO-agency copy, fabricate comparisons, or promise results. Include no price or free trial unless I specify one. Sign Michael / DAB.
>
> **Deliverable:** Create actual Gmail **drafts only** in the connected account(s) available and explicitly selected for the task. Do not send emails, schedule sends, or quietly draft to fallback generic inboxes. Aim for the requested count but **never manufacture contacts to fill a quota**. Skip weak leads and report any shortfall. Check accessible opt-out/suppression and previous-contact records; do not draft to known opt-outs. De-duplicate within the run.
>
> **Report:** Give me number of researched businesses, number excluded for shared emails or insufficient verification, number of successful Gmail drafts, country/sector breakdown, and the key issues or opportunities encountered. Do not claim inbox delivery based on a sent or drafted item.
>
> **Optional specifics:** [PRICE/EXPLICIT TRIAL IF APPROVED; sub-industries; priority towns; which connected Gmail account; prospect preferences].

## Short version
`DAB: Draft [N] emails to [INDUSTRY] in [COUNTRY/REGION]. Named verified decision-makers with direct public business email addresses only. No info@/shared inboxes. Short researched messages, no criticism, no price unless specified. Follow the DAB GitHub workspace. Draft only, never send.`

## Quality gates
1. Confirm the chosen industry and geography.
2. Research a verifiable company, relevant person and direct business email.
3. Validate a specific observation; never claim to have checked Google review counts unless actually verified.
4. Respect known opt-outs and de-duplicate within the run.
5. Draft a relevant, concise message.
6. Save the draft and report only confirmed successes.

For a new audience or country, produce a small pilot before launching a major *sending* campaign. A request for many drafts does not authorize sending them.

## Common failure patterns to avoid
- Contacting shared role inboxes merely to reach a requested quota.
- Long, near-identical marketing essays.
- Subjects and openings that insult the recipient's current reputation.
- Unsupported claims about rankings or competitors.
- Unclear identity, invented sender details, or ambiguous promises.
- Sending before confirming the prospect fit and the email setup.
- Confusing low response rates with verified spam filtering.
